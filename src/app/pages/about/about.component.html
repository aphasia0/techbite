I'm Antonio Giordano, a full stack developer from Italy. I have spent most of my career building web applications with Java, Spring and Angular, and I occasionally wander into Web3, Flutter and developer tooling.

Techbite is my personal notebook. I write down the things I learn while working: patterns that finally clicked, tools that save time, experiments that failed and the occasional opinion about where the industry is going. The goal is simple — short, practical notes that I would have wanted to find when I ran into the same problem.

I care about readable code, accessible interfaces and software that stays easy to maintain after the first release. When I'm not at a keyboard I'm probably reading about yet another JavaScript framework or trying to make a small app for someone who needs it.
